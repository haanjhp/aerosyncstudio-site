FlightRadar Watch Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 6, 2026

Overview

FlightRadar Watch uses your Apple Watch location and heading to show nearby aircraft on a radar-style display. The app does not require an account and does not sell personal information.

Location Data

The app uses location data to request nearby aircraft data and orient the radar display. Location coordinates may be included in network requests to the aircraft data provider so nearby aircraft can be returned. Location data is not used by the app to identify you, is not linked to an account, and is not stored by FlightRadar Watch.

Third-Party Aircraft Data

Aircraft information is provided by ADSB.lol open data under ODbL 1.0. Requests to third-party services may be subject to their own server logs and policies.

Diagnostics

The app may use Apple-provided diagnostics and crash reporting if enabled on your device. These reports help improve reliability and are handled by Apple according to Apple privacy settings.

Contact

Questions can be sent to support@aerosyncstudio.com.
